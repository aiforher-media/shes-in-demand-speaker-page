The 5 Talk Themes

Each day of the summit focuses on one pillar of demand-driven visibility. Choose the theme that best fits your expertise.

Day 1: Messaging That Magnetizes

When your positioning is dialed in, it does the selling for you. You stop convincing and start attracting the right, ready-to-buy clients.

Suggested topics
  • The one-line positioning statement that tripled my close rate (and how I found it)
  • Why your offer story matters more than your offer, and how to write one that makes the sale feel obvious
  • The niche shift that changed everything: from "I'm a solution to your problem" to "I'm THE solution"
  • How to use your voice, expertise, and taste as a moat nobody can copy
  • How I used AI to create a copywriting agent in my voice
  • The messaging mistake that was costing me clients (and the fix that brought them back)

Day 2: Visibility Plays That Compound

Strategies that keep working long after you hit publish: podcasts (hosting or guesting), speaking, press, partnerships, and getting recommended by AI itself.

Suggested topics
  • Podcast guesting as a lead-gen machine: how to pick the right shows, pitch yourself, and convert listeners
  • I started a podcast and it became my #1 client source, and AI handles 80% of the production
  • The partnership strategy that put me in front of 10,000 buyers without spending a dollar on ads
  • How to get recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude (AEO strategies that actually work)
  • How I landed my first major press feature (and turned it into $50K in inbound leads)
  • From invisible to booked: how I used speaking (stages, guest workshops, free trainings) to fill my pipeline
  • Using AI to repurpose one podcast episode into 10 pieces of content that drive visibility across platforms

Day 3: Content & Conversion Systems

From the post to the opt-in to the sale: content that attracts buyers, lead magnets that convert, and funnels that nurture cold leads into ready-to-buy clients.

Suggested topics
  • The AI-powered lead magnet that converts at 47% (and took me 2 hours to build)
  • How to write content that attracts buyers instead of browsers
  • The email sequence I wrote once and still runs on autopilot, 18 months later, still converting
  • One core asset, seven platforms: how I repurpose content so it works while I sleep
  • The webinar I've run 12 times that still sells (and the exact structure I use every time)
  • SEO that actually brings clients: the blog post that ranks #1 and sends me leads every single week
  • How I turned LinkedIn into my #1 client source (the content strategy that fills my pipeline on repeat)

Day 4: Paid Visibility & Ads

Organic is powerful. Paid is faster. Meta ads, LinkedIn ads, retargeting, AI platform ads, and how to run them profitably without burning budget or sanity.

Suggested topics
  • The Meta ad that finally worked after 10 failed attempts (and what I learned from the failures)
  • How I run profitable LinkedIn ads for a service business without a massive budget or marketing team
  • My entire ad testing process: how to find the winner without spending $5K to learn what doesn't work
  • Retargeting for service providers: how to turn cold scroll into warm leads (and then into buyers)
  • The ad copy framework that beats "guru swipe files" every time (because it's actually mine)
  • Using AI to write, test, and optimize ad creative (without losing the thing that makes it convert)
  • The new frontier: running ads on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI platforms (and whether it's worth it yet)

Day 5: Systems & Scale

Visibility without infrastructure is chaos. This is the unsexy backend that makes inbound leads turn into actual clients: the systems that catch attention, nurture interest, and convert without you chasing.

Suggested topics
  • The automated funnel that turns a single visibility play into months of inbound leads
  • How I built a lead nurture system that converts cold contacts into buyers (while I sleep)
  • How I turned my expertise into an AI-powered app that 10X'd my revenue (and became a new offer)
  • How I built a referral system that makes clients my best marketing channel (40% of new business on autopilot)
  • The content repurposing system that keeps me visible across 5 platforms without creating anything new
